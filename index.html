AutoPost Bot

AutoPost Bot is a personal desktop tool that creates short vertical videos (funny stories, jokes and educational facts) and publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account using the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. The tool writes a short script and turns it into speech.
  2. It assembles a 9:16 video with background footage and subtitles.
  3. After the account owner signs in with TikTok and grants permission, the video is uploaded to their own TikTok account.

Videos are labeled as AI-generated content.
